Overnight White Bread — Michael's Notes

A transcription of the handwritten one-loaf notes included with the family archive copy of the recipe.

One-loaf formula
  • 500 grams white flour — about 3 7/8 cups
  • 390 grams water — about 1 2/3 cups, 90°F to 95°F
  • 11 grams fine sea salt — 2 teaspoons
  • 0.4 gram instant dried yeast — about 1/8 teaspoon
Simple schedule
  • Mix around 7 p.m.
  • Shape around 8 a.m.
  • Bake around 9:15 a.m.
  • Out of the oven around 10 a.m.
More detailed working notes
  • Begin the autolyse around 7 p.m.; mix around 7:30–7:35 p.m.
  • Fold around 8 p.m., 8:30 p.m., and 9 p.m.
  • Shape around 8–9 a.m.
  • Proof to roughly 9:15 or 10:15 a.m., depending on the morning schedule.
  • Preheat around 8:30 or 9:30 a.m.
  • The handwritten bake note says 30 minutes covered and 15 minutes open.
  • A margin note points to page 91 of the cookbook for the refrigerated 10-hour proof.

These are family working notes, not a replacement for the published cookbook directions. The interactive recipe page keeps Ken Forkish's source formula and method as the default.
