Rustic Herb Chicken Stew

Rosemary • Thyme • Sage | White beans & hominy · 10–12 hearty servings

This transcription preserves the ingredient list, directions, and 5:00 PM target from the original US Letter cooking sheets prepared before the first batch. Notes from the first actual cooking appear on the interactive recipe page.

Shopping list

  • 1 whole young chicken (3½–5 lb)
  • 1 small pack fresh rosemary
  • 1 small pack fresh thyme
  • 1 small pack fresh sage
  • 1 large yellow onion
  • 1 bulb garlic (use 6 cloves)
  • 1 bag frozen mirepoix (12–16 oz)
  • 8 oz pre-sliced cremini mushrooms (optional)
  • 3 cans white hominy (15–16 oz each)
  • 2 cans white beans (15 oz each)
  • 2 bay leaves
  • About 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • Apple cider vinegar (1–2 tsp; optional)
  • 10–12 cups water for stock
  • Optional crusty bread, parsley or thyme

Original 5:00 PM schedule

12:30 Start stock
12:50–1:00 Gentle simmer begins
2:20–2:45 Chicken cooked; pull meat
2:45–3:30 Return bones to stock
3:30–3:45 Strain stock
3:45–4:00 Sauté vegetables
4:00–4:35 Simmer stew
4:35–5:00 Chicken, season, serve

Part 1 · Make the herbed chicken stock

12:30 · Load the pot. Place 1 whole chicken (3½–5 lb), 1 quartered onion, 6 smashed garlic cloves, 2 bay leaves, 1 rosemary sprig, 3 thyme sprigs, and 2 sage leaves in an 8–10 qt pot. Add 10–12 cups water, just enough to mostly cover. Do not rinse raw chicken.
12:50 · Cook low and slow. Bring to a gentle simmer, partially cover, and cook about 75–100 minutes. Avoid a rolling boil. Chicken must reach 165°F in the thickest breast and thigh. Add a little water only if needed to keep it mostly submerged.
2:30 · Pull the meat; enrich the stock. Cool chicken enough to handle and shred the meat into hearty pieces. Refrigerate the meat while the bones and cartilage return to the pot to simmer 30–45 minutes more. Skip the extra simmer if necessary.

Part 2 · Build the thick herb stew

3:30 · Strain and measure. Remove bones, herb stems, and aromatics. Strain the stock. Aim for 9–10 cups finished stock; add water if short, or reduce if there is too much. Return stock to a clean vessel.
3:45 · Sauté the easy vegetables. Heat 2 Tbsp olive oil in the empty pot. Add 1 bag frozen mirepoix (12–16 oz) and optionally 8 oz sliced cremini mushrooms; sauté 8–12 minutes. Blend 1 drained can white beans with 1 cup stock until creamy.
4:00 · Turn it into a stew. Add remaining stock, blended beans, 1 can whole drained white beans, and 3 cans drained hominy. Add 1 tsp finely minced fresh rosemary, 1 Tbsp fresh thyme leaves, and 3 finely chopped sage leaves. Simmer gently 25–35 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4:35 · Return chicken. Stir in shredded chicken; heat gently until steaming throughout, about 10–15 minutes. If too thick, add stock or water a little at a time. Taste for salt and black pepper.
4:50 · Finish and serve. If needed, add 1–2 tsp apple cider vinegar for brightness without making the broth sour. Serve at 5 PM with optional crusty bread or fresh herbs. No noodles, rice, or dumplings.
Freeze for lunches: Cool promptly in shallow containers or an ice bath; refrigerate within two hours. Freeze in 1½–2 cup portions. Best quality within about three months. Reheat to 165°F.
