Same-Day Straight Pizza Dough — Family Notes

The family rhythm that has grown around Ken Forkish's same-day pizza dough.

Pizza night starts in the morning

Michael likes to get this dough started early in the day. Once the flour and water are mixed and the dough begins its slow rise, the family already knows what is coming that evening. Watching it ferment through the afternoon creates its own kind of anticipation — family pizza night has begun hours before anyone chooses a topping.

A schedule that fits the tradition
  • The source's sample schedule is a natural match for family pizza night: mix around 10 a.m., shape the dough balls around 4 p.m., and make pizza anytime after 6 p.m.
  • The source also notes that the dough may be refrigerated overnight and used the next day, with more flavor developing during the extra cold time.
  • Tightly covered dough may be kept refrigerated for up to 2 days.
