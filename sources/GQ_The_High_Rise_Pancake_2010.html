The High-Rise Pancake

The GQ magazine article that helped start Dan’s German Pancake tradition, found and photographed by Cheryl in October 2026.

Publication: GQ, December 2010 Page: 156 Writer: Adam Rapoport Photo: Romulo Yanes

How we identified it

The original magazine page is headed The Technique > The High-Rise Pancake. Its recipe is titled The Big Pancake. Cheryl kept the clipping, and the publication date is printed at the bottom of the photographed page: December 2010. This confirms the family recollection that Michael passed a GQ pancake recipe on to Dan years ago.
