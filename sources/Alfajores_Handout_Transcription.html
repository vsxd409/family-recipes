Alfajores Recipe

A faithful transcription of the single-page alfajores handout Jude shared. The original handout describes alfajores as popular caramel sandwich cookies in Argentina.

Active time: 30 minutesTotal time: 1 hour 20 minutesYield: 2 dozen alfajores

Original ingredients

  • 1½ cups flour
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 2½ cups cornstarch
  • 1½ cups unsalted butter
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 5 egg yolks
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 jar (12 ounces) dulce de leche
  • ½ cup shredded coconut

Original method

  1. Whisk the flour, baking soda, baking powder, and cornstarch together; set aside.
  2. In a separate bowl, cream the butter and sugar until fluffy.
  3. Add egg yolks one at a time, mixing each individually. Mix in vanilla extract.
  4. Add the dry mixture to the butter mixture. Gently mix until dough forms. Roll into a ball, wrap in plastic, and chill 30 minutes.
  5. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  6. Roll dough to ¼-inch thickness. Cut circles with a 2-inch round cookie cutter.
  7. Bake on a parchment-lined baking sheet for 7–10 minutes. Cool on a wire rack.
  8. Spread a teaspoon of dulce de leche on the flat side of a cookie and top with another cookie to make a sandwich.
  9. Roll the sides of the sandwiches in coconut. Repeat for the remaining cookies.
