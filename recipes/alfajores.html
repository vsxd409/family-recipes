Jude takes Spanish lessons with Ms. Siri, who lived in Argentina for several years. She brings that experience into her teaching, introducing students to Spanish and culture beyond Spain, including Argentina. Alfajores, the dulce de leche sandwich cookies described in Jude’s handout, are a fitting addition to this part of his Spanish learning.

Method

Source and variations

The original one-page handout calls for a 30-minute dough chill and a 7–10-minute bake at 350°F, yielding two dozen alfajores. View the original source scan (PDF) or read the transcription. Toasted coconut, chocolate drizzle, and orange zest are creative additions, not part of the classroom handout.

Dietary note

The base recipe contains wheat flour, egg yolks, butter, and dulce de leche, so it is neither gluten-free nor dairy-free as written. Check package labels if cooking for someone with allergies.
