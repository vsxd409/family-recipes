Family story

Some recipes become family traditions without anyone quite planning it. Mallory and Chase both attended the nursery school at Grace Lutheran Church in Northbrook, Illinois, where families shared recipes in The Mustard Seed Cookbook. That little cookbook included Grandma Bubbe’s Apple Slices, with Chase’s name on the printed page.

Cheryl shared that Dan now makes these apple slices at Christmastime. What began as a recipe in a nursery-school cookbook has become a familiar part of the family’s Christmas celebrations.

Two versions of a family favorite

The quantities and instructions below follow the handwritten family recipe. The original printed cookbook version has a smaller crust formula, 4–6 apples instead of 6–8, and the same filling and glaze amounts. We’re keeping the familiar handwritten version as the default.
