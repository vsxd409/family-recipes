Same-Day Straight Pizza Dough

Source: Ken Forkish, Flour Water Salt Yeast, “Same-Day Straight Pizza Dough”
Makes: 5 dough balls
Family story

For Michael, family pizza night starts long before the oven comes on. Getting this dough mixed in the morning means everyone knows what is waiting at the end of the day. As it slowly ferments through the afternoon, the anticipation seems to rise right along with it — by evening, choosing toppings and making pizzas feels like the payoff to something the family has been looking forward to for hours.

Like the Overnight White Bread, this recipe comes from Ken Forkish's Flour Water Salt Yeast. It keeps the same wonderfully spare formula: flour, water, salt, and yeast, with time doing most of the work.

Method

From the source
  • The source recipe makes five approximately 340-gram dough balls. Each is intended for about a 12-inch thin-crust pizza-stone pizza or a thicker iron-skillet pizza.
  • The source gives a sample schedule of mixing at 10 a.m., shaping dough balls at 4 p.m., and making pizza anytime after 6 p.m.
  • The dough itself contains no olive oil. The method uses only a light coating of oil after the fold to help prevent sticking.
  • The source says refrigerated dough may be kept tightly covered for up to 2 days and notes that the next-day dough develops more flavor.
