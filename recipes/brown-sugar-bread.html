Dairy-free note — check labels

The bread-machine base uses water and neutral oil rather than milk or butter. The listed herb, spice, seed, fruit, and nut add-ins do not require dairy, but verify any packaged add-ins and avoid milk cross-contact in the bread machine.

Method

    During the first knead, look for a smooth, elastic, slightly tacky dough ball. If it smears heavily, add flour 1 tablespoon at a time. If it is stiff or dry, add water 1 teaspoon at a time.
