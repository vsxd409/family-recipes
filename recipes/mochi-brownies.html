Recipe notes
  • The source notes that whole milk may be replaced with other milk substitutes; different milks may make the brownies more or less rich.
  • Natural or Dutch-process cocoa may be used.
  • Cool completely before cutting; while warm, the mochi is very sticky and difficult to slice.
  • Store uncut mochi in a sealed container at room temperature up to 2–3 days.
