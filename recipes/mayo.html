Source & kitchen notes
  • The handwritten card calls for 1 large egg, 1 Tbsp Dijon mustard, 1 Tbsp red/white wine vinegar, 1/4 tsp salt, 1 tsp lemon juice, and 1 cup oil.
  • Its method is simply: mix the first five ingredients with an immersion blender, then slowly add the oil while blending.
  • Added safety note: because the base recipe uses a raw whole egg, use a pasteurized egg if raw-egg risk is a concern and keep the finished mayonnaise refrigerated.
