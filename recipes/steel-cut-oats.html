Gluten-free option — ingredient requirements

Gluten-free option, not automatically gluten-free. Use steel-cut oats specifically labeled gluten-free because ordinary oats may have cross-contact with wheat, barley, or rye. Check Smart Balance and other packaged ingredients. For the Savory Sesame option, use labeled gluten-free tamari instead of ordinary soy sauce, which often contains wheat.
