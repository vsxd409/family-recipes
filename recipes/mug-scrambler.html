Family story

This recipe comes from a cookbook Mallory and Michael found at Barnes & Noble — one of those happy bookstore finds that ended up getting used far more than anyone might have expected. It is full of simple recipes their children have been able to make for themselves since they were very young, giving them an early bit of independence in the kitchen.

This mug scrambler became Leo's favorite: quick, warm, a little sweet, and easy enough to make when breakfast is in his own hands.

Method

Cook's tip — from the source
  • The scrambler puffs up and fills the mug while cooking, then falls quickly once removed from the microwave.
  • Use a large 15-ounce microwave-safe mug; wider, shorter mugs work best and help reduce the chance of overflow. A 2-cup glass measure may also be used.
  • Microwave wattages vary, so adjust the cooking time as needed and cook just until the egg is set.
Site note for variations

Extra fruit or other add-ins can change the cooking time. After the source timing, continue in short microwave intervals only as needed until the egg is fully set.
