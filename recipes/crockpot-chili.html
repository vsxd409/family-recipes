Family story

I had been making this chili for years when, in 2010, Jacksonville’s community newspaper, The Source, put out a call for family recipes. I sent mine in, and it was selected for publication — giving this everyday family favorite a little moment in the spotlight.

The black olives have always been the controversial part. Some people insist they don’t belong in chili. I respectfully disagree. In this recipe, the black olives aren’t optional; they’re part of what makes the chili the chili.

Gluten-free note — check labels

Gluten-free as written when the packaged ingredients check out. Verify the canned beans and tomatoes, chili powder, other seasoning blends, Tabasco sauce, and olives. The optional chipotle-in-adobo must also be checked. Prevent cross-contact in cooking and serving.

Dairy-free note — check labels

The base chili contains no milk, cream, butter, or cheese. Check seasoning blends, canned ingredients, and sauces, especially optional chipotle in adobo. Keep cheese and sour cream toppings separate if serving dairy-free.

Michael's notes
