Approx. yield: 11 servings
First batch — October 23, 2026

Michael made this stew for the first time and thought it was delicious. The stock had plenty of flavor, but the overall result was more chicken-forward than expected; rosemary, thyme, and sage were present but quite subtle. The broth was also thinner than intended.

The bones simmered longer than the original plan called for. Quite a bit of water evaporated, so Michael added some back in — possibly a little more than the stew needed. It is not yet clear how much the extended simmer contributed to the balance of herbs.

Things to try next time — not yet tested
  • Bring the herbs forward: reserve some chopped thyme and a little rosemary or sage for the final few minutes, rather than relying only on the long simmer. Start with a small amount, taste, and increase gradually; rosemary and sage can dominate.
  • Get a thicker broth: measure the finished strained stock before adding water. Try keeping 8–9 cups for the stew and add more only if needed. Alternatively, blend a little more of the white beans or reduce the stock uncovered before assembling.
  • Keep the chicken-rich base: the chicken stock was a success. These are possible refinements to the herb balance and texture, not corrections to the batch that was cooked.
Gluten-free note — check labels

Gluten-free as written: the stew contains homemade chicken stock, hominy, white beans, vegetables, and herbs, without wheat flour. Verify canned beans, hominy, and any seasoned frozen vegetables. Serve without regular wheat bread, and avoid cross-contact when cooking for someone with celiac disease.

Freezing and serving

Portion into roughly 1½–2 cups per container after cooling promptly. White beans and hominy freeze well with shredded chicken. For best quality, use frozen portions within around three months. Reheat thoroughly to 165°F. Crusty bread or a little fresh thyme make good finishing touches.

About this recipe

This is a new family recipe, developed for a whole young chicken and a low-effort, large-batch cooking day. The original plan deliberately uses frozen mirepoix, pre-sliced mushrooms if desired, and blended canned white beans to give body without flour, cream, noodles, or dumplings. The original quantities and timeline are retained; observations and possible changes are documented separately.
