Recipe notes
  • The base recipe is enough to fill and frost a three-layer 8-inch cake.
  • After adding the butter, the frosting may look curdled. The source says to continue mixing for another 5 minutes at medium-low speed until it turns silky smooth.
  • The source notes that chocolate, espresso powder, fruit purées, caramel, and lemon zest are possible flavor variations.
